Assumptions & simplifications

  • 3 s internal cooldown is modeled exactly. The cooldown starts the moment a proc happens, and the first swing landing 3 s or more later can proc again. Blocks during the cooldown restore nothing. Server batching and latency are ignored.
  • "Capped" means 95% or more of the cooldown cap. The cap is the MP5 you'd get if the first swing after every cooldown procced. Unless every swing procs (100% effective block at Rank 3), MP5 only approaches the cap and never quite reaches it, so "capped" is defined as 95% or more. 80–95% shows as "near cap".
  • Swing timing. The formula assumes all enemies' swings are evenly staggered (exact for one enemy). The Monte Carlo gives each enemy a fixed timer with a random starting offset. Neither models swing-timer jitter, or enemies drifting in and out of sync during a fight.
  • Parry haste is excluded in both directions: your parries speeding up your swings, and enemies' parries speeding up theirs. The second one slightly increases incoming swings. To approximate it, set swing speed a little faster.
  • Single-roll attack table. Block sits after miss/dodge/parry, so avoidance can push block off the table. Crits and crushing blows come after block and only matter when that happens.
  • Inputs are final values against the target. No automatic penalty for enemies above your level (weapon skill vs. defense). Enter the avoidance and block you actually have against that enemy.
  • Holy Shield is recast the moment its cooldown ends. Each block uses a charge, including blocks during the 3 s Shield Specialization cooldown that restore nothing. The formula weights MP5 by uptime, which is an approximation. The Monte Carlo tracks charges and recasts exactly. Holy Shield's damage, mana cost and global cooldown are ignored. Redoubt and other temporary block buffs are not modeled.
  • The graph uses the formula, not the Monte Carlo, so it inherits the formula's evenly-staggered-swings assumption.
  • Every swing can be blocked. All incoming damage is assumed to be frontal melee auto-attacks. Attacks from behind (can't be blocked or parried), spells, ranged attacks and enemy special abilities are not modeled.
  • Enemies attack nonstop. No stuns, crowd control, fear, movement or deaths. Enemy count stays constant for the whole fight.
  • No mana overflow. Every proc counts as usable mana, as if you're never at full. In practice, procs while full are wasted.
  • Max mana is constant. Each proc restores 6% of the Max mana input. Buffs gained or lost mid-fight are ignored.
  • Steady-state average. MP5 is a long-run average. Short fights vary a lot, and the opening and ending of a fight are ignored.
  • Only the mana part of the talent is modeled. The +10/20/30% shield block value (damage absorbed) is ignored.
  • Comparison sources are constant MP5. Blessing of Wisdom has 100% uptime, the totem is always in range, and potions are used on every cooldown. Defaults are classic WoW values. Spirit-based regen and the five-second rule are excluded.
