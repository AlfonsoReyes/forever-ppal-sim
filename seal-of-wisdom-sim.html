Assumptions & simplifications

  • Default values are from WoW Classic. Seal mana per proc is 50 / 71 / 90 and Judgement mana per proc is 33 / 46 / 59 for Ranks 1 / 2 / 3. The Rank 2 Judgement value (46) was inferred from the progression and isn't confirmed. 15 PPM and a 50% Judgement chance are applied to every rank. WoW Forever may differ, so every value is editable.
  • Both effects are up 100% of the time. The seal and the Judgement debuff on the target are assumed always active. The time and global cooldowns spent judging and recasting the seal are ignored.
  • Mana costs are not subtracted. This is gross regen. Casting the seal and Judgement costs mana, so net regen is lower.
  • Only your melee hits are counted. In Classic, other players' attacks and spells on the judged target can also trigger Judgement of Wisdom, but only for the attacker's own mana. This page models only your auto-attacks.
  • Only landed hits can proc. Misses, dodges and parries can't. Blocked and glancing hits still land and can proc.
  • No special attacks. Only auto-attacks are counted. Instant melee abilities that can also trigger procs, and extra attacks such as Windfury or Reckoning, are not modeled.
  • Steady-state averages. MP5 is a long-run average. Mana overflow while full is ignored.
