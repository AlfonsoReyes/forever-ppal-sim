Formula

The level bonus, with player level \(L_p\) and enemy level \(L_e\):

A swing of yours that lands (chance \(h_p = 1 - A_e\)) creates or refreshes the shield. Only one shield exists at a time. An enemy swing that lands on you (chance \(h_e = 1 - A_p\)) absorbs the shield if one is up, and that's a proc. Swings come on fixed timers \(S_p\) and \(S_e\), and each hit/miss roll is independent.

Take an enemy hit that lands. The shield is missing only if every swing you made since the previous landed enemy hit missed. That previous hit was \(j\) enemy swings back with probability \(h_e(1-h_e)^{j-1}\). In those \(jS_e\) seconds you swung \(\lfloor x_j \rfloor\) or \(\lfloor x_j \rfloor + 1\) times, depending on how the two timers line up, where \(x_j = jS_e/S_p\). Averaging over that offset:

Assumptions & simplifications

  • Every enemy hit fully absorbs the shield. The shield is assumed small enough that any landed enemy auto-attack consumes all of it, so every hit that finds a shield procs. A partially absorbed shield does not proc and is not modeled.
  • Only your swings that land create a shield. If the enemy dodges or parries a swing, or you miss, no shield is created or refreshed. Seal of Fury is assumed active 100% of the time.
  • Only enemy hits that land consume the shield. If an enemy swing misses you or you dodge or parry it, the shield stays up for the next hit. Blocked hits still land, so they consume the shield and proc.
  • Avoidance is a flat, independent roll on every swing. Enter the final values against this enemy, including level-based miss chance. Enemy block is not part of "enemy avoidance", since a blocked swing still lands and creates the shield.
  • One shield at a time. A new swing refreshes the shield rather than stacking a second one. The shield is assumed not to expire before an enemy hits.
  • Single attacker. For several enemies, enter their combined hit interval as the enemy swing speed (e.g. 3 enemies at 2.0 s → 0.67 s). The level bonus uses the single enemy level you enter.
  • Fixed swing timers. The formula is exact for fixed timers with a random starting offset between the two. Timer jitter mid-fight is not modeled.
  • Parry haste is excluded in both directions.
  • Level bonus. +15% per level the attacker is above you, capped at +45% (3 or more levels). Lower-level attackers give no penalty.
  • Mana per proc is the base value. Enter the tooltip's X before the level bonus. Any other modifiers are not modeled.
  • No mana overflow. Every proc counts as usable mana, as if you're never at full. Steady-state long-run average.
