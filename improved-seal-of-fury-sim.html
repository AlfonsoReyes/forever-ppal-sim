Formula

The level bonus, with player level \(L_p\) and enemy level \(L_e\):

Each of your auto-attacks creates a shield, and the next enemy hit fully absorbs it. Only one shield exists at a time, so a new swing refreshes it rather than stacking. With player swing speed \(S_p\) and enemy swing speed \(S_e\), procs happen at whichever rate is slower:

If you swing faster (\(S_p \le S_e\)), there is always a fresh shield waiting, so every enemy hit procs. If the enemy swings faster, every shield you create is consumed before your next swing, and enemy hits in between find no shield. With fixed swing timers this holds exactly for any phase offset.

Assumptions & simplifications

  • Every enemy hit fully absorbs the shield. The shield is assumed small enough that any landed enemy auto-attack consumes all of it, so every hit that finds a shield procs. A partially absorbed shield does not proc and is not modeled.
  • Every one of your auto-attacks creates a shield. Seal of Fury is assumed active 100% of the time. If only landed hits create a shield, divide your swing speed by your hit chance (e.g. 2.6 s at 90% hit → 2.9 s).
  • Every enemy swing lands. Your dodges, parries and misses aren't modeled; they don't hit, so they can't consume a shield. To account for them, raise the enemy swing speed: effective speed = speed ÷ (1 − avoidance), e.g. 2.0 s at 20% avoidance → 2.5 s. Blocked hits still land and are assumed to consume the shield.
  • One shield at a time. A new swing refreshes the shield rather than stacking a second one. The shield is assumed not to expire before an enemy hits.
  • Single attacker. For several enemies, enter their combined hit interval as the enemy swing speed (e.g. 3 enemies at 2.0 s → 0.67 s). The level bonus uses the single enemy level you enter.
  • Fixed swing timers. The min-rate formula is exact for fixed timers. With random jitter, when the two speeds are close, some enemy hits occasionally land before a new shield appears, so real MP5 is slightly lower.
  • Parry haste is excluded in both directions.
  • Level bonus. +15% per level the attacker is above you, capped at +45% (3 or more levels). Lower-level attackers give no penalty.
  • Mana per proc is the base value. Enter the tooltip's X before the level bonus. Any other modifiers are not modeled.
  • No mana overflow. Every proc counts as usable mana, as if you're never at full. Steady-state long-run average.
